The Abhimanyu lesson

Half-learned is not trained

Abhimanyu heard how to enter the Chakravyuha while still in his mother's womb, but the lesson on how to exit was never finished. On the 13th day of the Kurukshetra war, that gap in his training trapped him.

Thick manuals and long lectures leave the same kind of gaps. A jawan may read the procedure, but has never done it under pressure. AI changes this by turning each manual into a short, repeatable loop that tells the instructor exactly where every trainee needs more practice.
